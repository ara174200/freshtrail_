How it works

Three scans. One honest journey.

01

Tag

The farmer logs a batch and prints a QR label for the crate.

02

Track

The wholesaler logs the crate, splits it into weighed lots, and each mandi vendor scans their own lot.

03

Show

The consumer scans the same QR and sees the whole journey. No login needed.
